Dashboard

Users

New sign-ups wait as pending until you approve them.

Approve gives someone access right away. Disable locks them out immediately and keeps their data, so you can re-enable them later. Delete removes their profile and journal data for good. Their login itself stays in Supabase Authentication until you remove it there.

Error log

Problems people ran into on the site. Kept for 30 days.

Crash: the code stopped with an error. Failed load: an image, script or style file couldn't be loaded, often a missing upload. App error: something the app caught and logged, like a failed save. Warning: worth a look, but the app kept working. Reports never include what people typed, their journal or their screenshots.

Invite a user

Send them your sign-up link. Once they create an account, they'll show up here under Pending for you to approve.

Prefer to create the account yourself? Add them in Supabase → Authentication → Users. They'll appear here as pending too.
