Features

Everything you need to review your trading

Built around how futures and prop firm traders actually work, from the first evaluation to the payout.

Calendar

Every trading day at a glance

Your month on one calendar, with each day's P&L and number of trades. The stats, trade score and cumulative P&L line follow whichever month you pick.

  • Net P&L, profit factor, win rate and expectancy
  • Trading history with entry, exit, duration and result
  • Evening trades count toward the next trading day, like the futures session

Trade Journal

A page for every trade

Paste or drop in your chart, tag the trade Win or Loss, and note how you felt and how closely you followed your plan. Your pages sit in a gallery, with the day's numbers above them.

  • Up to 6 screenshots and notes on each page
  • Feeling and a 1 to 5 discipline rating, so patterns show up
  • Saves as you type

Trade Analytics

Find out what actually pays

See your results by weekday, month, setup and symbol, and compare longs with shorts. Stop guessing which setups are working and put your size where the edge is.

  • Best weekday, best month and best setup
  • Average winner and loser
  • Profit by symbol, from NQ and ES to gold

T1 News

Never get caught by red-folder news

Switch the calendar from P&L to T1 News and every high-impact release shows up on its day, with the time it comes out. Plan around the big numbers before the week starts.

  • CPI, jobs, PPI, retail sales, GDP and the Fed
  • The whole month at once, past weeks included
  • Shown in your own time zone

Economic Calendar

The whole week's macro events

Every event for the week, day by day, with its impact, forecast and previous number. Look ahead to the following weeks to see what's already scheduled.

  • Filter by high, medium and low impact
  • US events only, or every currency
  • Updates by itself while it's open

Payouts & Tax

Evals, payouts and taxes, sorted

Log what each evaluation, reset and activation costs you, and every payout you receive. Clarity Journal estimates the tax on each firm's profit and how much to set aside each quarter.

  • Payouts by firm and by month
  • US federal brackets, self-employment tax and your state rate
  • A Consistency Calculator to check your payout rules

Journal Card

Share your progress

Turn today, this week, this month or all time into a clean P&L card. Use the built-in design or your own background, then download it or copy it straight into a post.

  • Sharp, high-resolution images
  • Your background is saved for next time

And more

The details that save you time

CSV import

Bring in a broker or prop firm export (Tradovate and similar). Symbols, prices, times, fees and P&L are picked up for you.

On your phone too

Everything works in your phone's browser, with a menu and a calendar sized for small screens. Nothing to install.

Consistency Calculator

Check your best day against your firm's consistency rule before you ask for a payout.

Eval Tracker

Every evaluation fee, reset, activation, data feed and subscription in one list, by category and month.

Session-aware dates

A trade that starts at 6 PM or later is counted on the next trading day, so your days match the futures session.

Your data, your way

Download everything in your account as one file whenever you like, or delete your account in Settings.

Privacy

Your journal stays yours

Your trades and journal are stored under your own account, and the database itself only lets your account read or change them. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

  • Private by defaultAdmins approve accounts, but can't see your trades or journal in the app.
  • No ads, no trackingNo analytics or advertising tools, and your data is never sold.
  • Built with security in mindEncrypted connections, hashed passwords and protection against password guessing.
