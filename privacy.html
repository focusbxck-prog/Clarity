Clarity Journal

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

The short version: we collect only what's needed to run your journal. We don't use analytics, ads or tracking, and we never sell your data. Only your account can see your trades and journal, and you can download or delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Clarity Journal (clarityjournal.trade) is run by Clarity Journal. Contact us at FocusBxck@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username and password. Your password is stored securely hashed by our sign-in provider, so we never see it. We also keep your account status, when you signed up and when you agreed to our terms.
  • Failed sign-in attempts: the username tried and the time, so we can slow down password guessing. These are deleted after a day, or as soon as you sign in successfully.
  • What you add to your journal: your trades (symbols, prices, sizes, fees, profit and loss, times, account names, strategies and notes), journal pages and screenshots, Eval Tracker expenses, payouts, profile photo, and your calculator settings and preferences.
  • Error reports: if something goes wrong on the site, your browser sends us the error message, the page it happened on, your browser and device type, and which account was signed in (if any), so we can fix it. Error reports never include anything you type into the app, your trades, your journal, your screenshots or your password, and they're deleted after 30 days.
  • Technical information: when you use the Service, our hosting and database providers may record things like your IP address, browser type and request times. This is used for security and to keep the Service running.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, we don't collect payment details, and we don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

How we use it

We use your information to run the Service (saving and showing your journal), approve and manage accounts, keep the Service secure, reply when you contact us, and meet legal obligations. If you're in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are providing the Service you signed up for, our legitimate interest in keeping it secure, and complying with the law.

Who can see your data

Your trades, journal and other saved data can only be read or changed by your own account. That's enforced by our database's access rules, not just by the app.

We can see your username, email and account status so we can approve and manage accounts. The app doesn't give anyone else access to your trades or journal. Like any hosted service, anyone with full access to our database could technically reach stored data. We only do that when it's needed to fix a problem, when you ask us to, or when the law requires it.

Services we use

  • Supabase provides our database, sign-in and server functions, and stores your account and journal data.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts. Your browser downloads them from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr provide the charting and database code libraries. Your browser downloads them from these services, which receive your IP address.
  • Economic calendar data comes from the ForexFactory feed (Fair Economy) and is fetched by our server, so none of your data is sent. If our server can't reach the feed, your browser may fetch it through r.jina.ai, which then receives your IP address.
  • Our web host, which serves the website files and receives standard request information such as your IP address.

These providers may process data in other countries, including the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account in Settings removes your trades, journal and other saved data straight away. To also remove your sign-in details (email and username), contact us and we'll delete them. Our providers' backups may keep copies for a short time before they're overwritten.

Your rights

You can see and change your data in the app, download a copy at any time (Settings → Download my data), and delete it (Settings → Delete account). Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, correct it, restrict or object to how we use it, or complain to your local data protection authority. California residents: we don't sell or share personal information. To make a request, email FocusBxck@gmail.com.

Security

The Service uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords and database access rules that keep each account's data separate. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

Children

Clarity Journal isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use cookies for advertising or analytics. The site stores a few things in your browser's local storage because it needs them to work:

  • Keeping you signed in: your sign-in session from Supabase (a key starting with sb-).
  • Economic calendar: a saved copy of the latest calendar, so it still shows if the feed is down (clarityJournal:econCache).
  • Older data: earlier versions of Clarity Journal saved data in the browser (clarityJournal: keys). When you sign in, this is moved into your account once.

Because these are strictly necessary and aren't used to track you, we don't show a cookie banner. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings, though you'll be signed out. The font and code providers above don't set cookies on our site, but they do receive your IP address when your browser downloads their files.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top, and for important changes we'll let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email FocusBxck@gmail.com.
